About ClearLens

Last updated September 30, 2026

ClearLens is an independent news site focused on public health and health policy. Our goal is to help readers cut through noise by showing what different sources say about the same story.

How it works

ClearLens uses automated software to collect recent items from public sources such as government health agencies, medical literature databases like PubMed, and news outlets. AI language models then summarize each story and compare how multiple outlets and sources describe it. Articles are generated and published automatically, and are not individually written or reviewed by a human editor.

What that means for you

  • Summaries can contain mistakes, omissions, or misinterpretations of the original material.
  • Our "sources analyzed" and agree/dispute indicators are automated estimates, not verdicts on what is true.
  • ClearLens does not endorse any outlet, product, or organization mentioned.

Please read our Disclaimer before relying on anything here.

Corrections and contact

If you spot an error, or you are a rights holder or person named in an article and want it corrected or removed, email clearlens.live@gmail.com. We review requests promptly and correct or remove content when appropriate.
