Overview

ClearLens is an AI-sourced news platform. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Search queries — terms you enter in the search bar are used only to return matching articles.
  • Article submissions — if you submit or upload an article for editorial review, we store the text, metadata, and attachment you provide.
  • Basic analytics — our hosting provider may log standard request data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and operational purposes.

How we use information

  • To display articles, search results, and editorial submissions.
  • To improve our reporting and the reliability of our source analysis.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising.

AI and automation

Article text, source consensus summaries, and video briefs are generated with the assistance of AI models using public news sources. No personal information about you is used to generate content.

Cookies and storage

We use a session cookie solely to keep editorial staff logged in to the studio dashboard. Public visitors are not tracked with advertising cookies.

Your choices

  • You may browse the public site without providing personal information.
  • You may request removal of an article you submitted by contacting us.
  • You may clear your browser cookies at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the ClearLens editorial team through the channels listed on the home page.
